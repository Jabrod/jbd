# Changelog

All notable changes to JBD, newest first. Each version is a tag (v1.1.0, …) and a release on GitHub; the same notes are at jabrod.com/jbd/changelog and in the app under Help ▸ What's New.

## 1.1.0 · 16 October 2026

### The AI Director
- **Edit by describing what you want.** The Director plans the edit, works step by step on your timeline, and checks the result before it reports back. Every change can be undone.
- **Generate video and voiceover.** Choose a video model, generate shots with sound, and add narration on its own audio track. Regenerate a clip, or make a variation to compare.
- **It sees your edit.** The Director looks at rendered frames to check framing, titles and logos, and to find things inside a shot.
- **Logos that follow the action.** Place a logo on an object, such as a ball or a product, and it tracks the object as it moves. Solid backgrounds are removed automatically.
- **Titles that always fit.** Titles are measured with the real font and kept inside the frame.
- **Three modes:** Ask (approve each change), Auto edits, and Plan (proposes only).
- **Give it context.** Right-click a clip and choose Add to Director, or Ctrl+Alt+click it. Attach pictures and files to a message.
- **Chat history** in Director view, with a sidebar you can hide.

### Editor
- Right-click a track header to add or delete tracks.
- The mouse wheel scrolls tracks up and down; Shift+wheel scrolls through time; Ctrl+wheel zooms.
- Alt+click selects one side of a linked clip.
- Refined Dark and Light themes.
- Help menu: What's New, Check for Updates and Report a Problem.

### Fixes
- Long prompts no longer push the Director's input off screen.
- The Director's undo step never includes edits you make while it works.
- Clips still being generated when JBD closes can be fetched later.

## 1.0.0 · 8 October 2026
The first JBD built on FilmCraft: a new, faster editor.
- A new, faster editor: rebuilt timeline and preview, with ripple, roll, slip and slide trims.
- Colour grading with curves, wheels, looks and scopes; audio mixing with loudness meters.
- Titles and caption tracks, speech to text on your computer, and 4K export.
- Templates for YouTube, Reels, Shorts, square and 4:5, and the new start screen.

## 0.1.0 · 3 October 2026
The first JBD (an earlier app, since replaced): an AI Director with chat history and web research, motion graphics with custom keyframes, timeline ranges, clip looping, transitions, colour looks and export controls.
