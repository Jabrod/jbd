## Description
JBD is a desktop video editor for creators, made by Jabrod.

Start from a template for every shape you post (YouTube 16:9, Reels and Shorts 9:16, square and
4:5) and edit on a real multi-track timeline with the trims professionals use: ripple, roll, slip
and slide, with splits, speed changes, transitions, markers and unlimited undo.

Grade colour with curves, colour wheels and looks while scopes show the result. Mix sound with
track and clip mixers, EQ, denoise and a limiter, with loudness meters to broadcast standards. Add
titles with a real text engine, edit caption tracks, and import or export SRT and WebVTT captions.
Turn speech into text on your own computer.

Export H.264 video up to 4K, ProRes and more. A GPU compositor keeps playback smooth, and your
projects and media stay on your computer.

JBD is free with a Jabrod account: the whole editor, with exports up to 720p and a small
"Made with JBD" mark. New accounts get JBD Pro free for 7 days. JBD Pro (bought on jabrod.com)
unlocks 1080p and 4K exports without the mark and every export preset.

Coming next: the Director. Tell JBD what you want ("a 30-second reel, remove the pauses, add
Hinglish captions") and it edits your timeline one undoable step at a time.

## Features (up to 20, ≤ 200 characters each)
- Templates for YouTube, Reels, Shorts, square and 4:5 posts
